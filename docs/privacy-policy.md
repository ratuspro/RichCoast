# RichCoast — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-13

This policy covers the RichCoast mobile game (`com.richcoast.game`) on Google Play.

> **This document is not yet hosted.** It must be published at a stable public URL before the
> first Play release, and that URL entered in two places: the Play Console listing, and
> `PrivacyPolicy.Url` in `Assets/Game/Core/Analytics/PrivacyPolicy.cs`. Until then the in-game
> consent screen shows a short notice with no link, which is deliberate — a dead link in a
> compliance surface is worse than no link.

## The short version

RichCoast has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. It does not know who you are. If you allow it,
the game sends anonymous statistics about how it is played so it can be tuned. You can say no,
and you can change your mind later; the game is identical either way.

## What is stored on your device

The game saves, on your device only:

- your best score, best level, and how many runs you have played;
- your sound, haptics and privacy choices;
- one interrupted run, so you can continue it;
- a short rolling log of recent gameplay events, used for debugging.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## What is sent, if you allow it

Only if you choose **ALLOW** on the privacy screen, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics
through GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS). These describe the game, not you:

- when a run starts and ends, how long it lasted, and whether it ended by a ball crossing the
  death line or by running out of moves;
- the level reached, and how long each level took;
- how many balls were dropped, and how many struck the golden gate;
- taps on the trap door, and which of its nine positions was lit.

**What is never sent:** your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location,
advertising ID, or the contents of your device. Nothing collected identifies you, and nothing is
sold or shared for advertising.

GameAnalytics assigns a random identifier to an installation so that events from one play session
can be counted together. It is not linked to you or to any account, and it is discarded when you
uninstall.

## Choosing, and changing your mind

You are asked once, the first time you open the game. Neither answer is preselected and the game
is fully playable either way.

To change the answer later, open **PRIVACY** on the title screen. Turning collection off stops it
immediately and deletes the local event log. Data already sent is anonymous and aggregated and
cannot be traced back to you to remove; uninstalling the app stops all collection permanently.

## Children

RichCoast is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Third parties

- **GameAnalytics ApS** — analytics, only with your consent: <https://gameanalytics.com/privacy>
- **Google Play** — distribution and crash reporting, governed by Google's own policy.
